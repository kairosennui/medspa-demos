The Baywood Difference

Cosmetic medicine, practised by doctors.

Baywood Laser & Cosmetic Clinic has served downtown Toronto from 1033 Bay Street since 2001. Every treatment plan begins with a proper medical consultation, and injectable and laser procedures are delivered under physician direction — not in a retail spa setting.

From a first Botox appointment to EVLT varicose vein treatment, patients see the same trusted clinical team from consultation through follow-up.
