The Vimi Difference

Laser medicine and skincare, under one roof since 1993.

Vimi Laser & Skincare Clinic has served the Markham Rd and Bur Oak community for more than 30 years, with a treatment menu that spans medical-grade laser work, injectables, body contouring and facials — all in one clinic.

Care is directed by licensed medical staff, including practitioner Dr. Donna Birbrager, and the clinic has delivered over 100,000 treatments to patients across Markham and the wider GTA.
